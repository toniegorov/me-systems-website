01 / Approach

Data that supports decisions

ME Systems is an engineering and software platform that turns real vehicle data into context for decision-making. A vehicle constantly produces signals: powertrain and electronic system data, pedal positions, speed, coordinates, dashboard warnings and other parameters.

On their own, these signals rarely answer the key questions: what is happening to the vehicle, how much does it matter and what should happen next?

02 / Platform

One engineering platform

ME Systems brings together hardware, software, navigation data and AI processing. The platform collects data, connects it with operating conditions, event history and human actions, then helps determine the next step.

This technology powers two products: ME Race for driver development and session analysis, and ME Fleet for proactive vehicle health management.
