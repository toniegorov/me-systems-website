What happens to the data you enter

Clicking “Download enquiry” creates a text file in your browser with your name, email, selected topic and message. The form contents are not sent to a server or saved in the website’s storage.

Your saved file

The file stays on your device and can be deleted from your downloads folder. Until enquiry delivery is connected and a privacy policy is approved, the form operates only in this mode.
